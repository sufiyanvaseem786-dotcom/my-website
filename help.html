Frequently Asked Questions

How can I place an order?

Open a product, select the required quantity and use the available order option. You may need to log in or create an account before completing checkout.

How can I check my order?

Open your Account section to view available order information.

What if I receive a wrong or damaged product?

Contact S.A Power Tools as soon as possible. Eligible cases may be handled through the replacement policy.

How can I contact customer support?

Open the Customer Support page from the Settings menu or use the support option provided on this website.
