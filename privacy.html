Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

S.A Power Tools respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our website, create an account, place an order or contact us.

Important: We only intend to collect information that is reasonably required to provide our website, account, ordering, delivery and customer support services.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Mobile phone number
  • Delivery address
  • Order and product information
  • Account information
  • Information you provide when contacting customer support

2. Account Information

When you create an account on S.A Power Tools, information such as your name, email address or mobile number may be associated with your account.

This information may be used to provide account access, order management and customer support.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information may be used for purposes including:

  • Creating and managing your customer account
  • Processing and managing orders
  • Providing delivery services
  • Confirming orders
  • Providing customer support
  • Responding to customer enquiries
  • Improving website functionality and services
  • Preventing fraudulent or unauthorized activity

4. Order Information

When you place an order, information required to fulfil the order may be stored or processed, including your name, phone number, email, delivery address, selected products and order amount.

This information may be used to process, confirm, deliver and support your order.

5. Communication

We may contact you using the contact information you provide for purposes related to your account, orders, delivery, replacement requests or customer support.

Communication may occur through available channels such as phone, WhatsApp or email.

6. Payment Information

Payment processing may involve third-party payment or financial service providers.

S.A Power Tools does not ask customers to provide confidential information such as UPI PINs or banking passwords through the website.

Customers should never share their OTP, UPI PIN, password or banking credentials with anyone claiming to represent S.A Power Tools.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

The website may use browser technologies such as cookies or local storage to provide features such as shopping cart functionality, login-related preferences, order information or website settings.

These technologies may store information on your device to help the website function properly.

8. Third-Party Services

S.A Power Tools may use third-party services for functions such as website hosting, authentication, payment processing, analytics or communication.

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

9. Google Account Login

If Google Sign-In is available on the website and you choose to use it, authentication may be handled through Google's authentication services.

S.A Power Tools receives only the information made available through the authentication process and uses it for account-related purposes.

10. Account Security

We take reasonable steps to protect customer information. However, no online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Customers are responsible for keeping their passwords, OTPs and account credentials confidential.

11. Data Sharing

We do not intend to sell customer personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to provide services such as hosting, authentication, payment processing, delivery or customer communication.

12. Legal Requirements

Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law, regulation, legal process or a valid governmental request.

13. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for general customers and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

14. External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites or services. S.A Power Tools is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

15. Your Information

If you have questions about your personal information, account, orders or privacy, you can contact S.A Power Tools using the contact details provided on our website.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, services or legal requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

17. Contact Us

S.A Power Tools

For privacy questions, account-related concerns or requests regarding your information, please contact S.A Power Tools through the contact details available on our website.
